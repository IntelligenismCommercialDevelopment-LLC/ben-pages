Compare two lists, then hand off a checkable result

This page performs one task: it compares two plain multi-line lists of strings in this browser, delivers three copyable final lists (only in A, only in B, common to both) under explicit rules, and prepares an editable plain-text handoff you can take away. Whether this task is useful to anyone is an open question this page does not answer.
