Infrastructure fixture with one tool written here: a browser-local list comparison. Paste two plain multi-line lists (one string per line) and it produces three copyable final lists — only in A, only in B, common to both — plus an editable plain-text handoff with the rules and per-side counts. Everything runs in your browser; list contents are never uploaded.
